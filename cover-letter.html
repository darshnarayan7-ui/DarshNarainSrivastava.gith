Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to introduce myself as an emerging photographer drawn to the spaces where everyday life becomes quietly cinematic. My work begins with curiosity: a street just after rain, a monument under artificial light, a face caught between movement and stillness, or a small animal and a flower that reward a closer look.

The photographs in my portfolio move across urban life, heritage, wildlife, people, and botanical detail. I am interested in the tension between scale and intimacy—how a large structure can feel personal, how a fleeting gesture can hold a whole story, and how light can turn an ordinary scene into a place worth remembering.

I am continuing to develop a visual language built on strong framing, thoughtful color, and patient observation. I bring a willingness to learn, a respectful approach to the people and places I photograph, and the discipline to edit a body of work into a clear point of view.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute this perspective to editorial, documentary, cultural, travel, or creative projects. Thank you for taking the time to view my work. I would be glad to speak about how I could support your next visual story.

Warm regards,

Darsh Narayan
