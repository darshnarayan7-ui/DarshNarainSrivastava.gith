01 / THE STORY

Curiosity first.
Camera second.

This portfolio is a record of learning to pay attention. I photograph the places and details that shape everyday memory: a lit monument after dark, a commuter crossing an empty road, an animal pausing between movements, or sunlight breaking through a canopy.

The work moves between documentary instinct and quiet observation. I am drawn to contrast—public and intimate, monumental and small, saturated color and near-black shadow—and to images that leave room for the viewer to stay a little longer.

— Darsh Narayan
